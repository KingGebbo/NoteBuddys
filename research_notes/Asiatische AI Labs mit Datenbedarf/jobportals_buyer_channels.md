# Job-Portal and LinkedIn Signals and Buyer Channels for Human-Data Demand from Asian AI Players (as of 12 Nov 2026)

Scope note: this covers cross-cutting job-portal and LinkedIn signals plus buyer channels, written for PathWize (an EU expert human-data vendor). Method: about 45 web searches and fetches in English, Chinese, Korean and Japanese. LinkedIn could only be reached through search-engine results. `site:linkedin.com` queries returned mostly mirror job boards (foundit, outscal, The Muse, TCV/Coatue portfolio boards), and those mirrors are the sources cited here. Each item is marked with how it was verified:
- **[opened]**: I fetched and read the page.
- **[snippet]**: I saw only a search snippet or a search-engine summary and did not open the page.

The web-search budget ran out before the last planned checks (a DeepSeek procurement posting and Chinese job titles inside ByteDance's "AI 数据与安全" unit). Those are listed under Gaps. No individual names from LinkedIn profiles are included.

## Q1. Which open or recent (2025–2026) roles show a data-buying function at Asian AI companies, and which teams and titles should PathWize target?

### Takeaway
The clearest and most numerous buyer signals come from **ByteDance's Seed "Global Data" team**, which job titles also call "AI Data and Safety". Its roles are titled *Partnership Development (Analyst/Specialist/Manager) – LLM Data Acquisition and Production* and *Product Manager – LLM Data Acquisition and Production*. They are based in Singapore, San Jose, Seattle, Los Angeles and San Francisco. They develop suppliers for SFT, RM, Eval, multimodal and agentic data, and most require Mandarin. Tencent is hiring a *Data Partnerships Lead* in its TEG Procurement & Sourcing function (Palo Alto/LA). TikTok's *AI Data Service & Operations (ADSO)* runs vendor-management roles in Singapore for annotation workforce vendors. Press from November 2026 reports that ByteDance and Tencent are centralising data purchasing in dedicated units (ByteDance "AI 数据与安全", Tencent "AI Data 部"). Those units are the strategic targets.

### Inferences
- **Primary outreach targets (titles):**
  1. ByteDance Seed Global Data / "AI Data and Safety": *Partnership Development Analyst/Specialist/Manager – LLM Data Acquisition and Production*, *Product Manager – LLM Data Acquisition and Production* (including Safety Domain), and the PM leads on "global data labeling vendors". Singapore is the hub, with US satellites in San Jose, Seattle, LA and SF.
  2. Tencent TEG *Data Partnerships Lead/Specialist* (Procurement & Sourcing, Palo Alto/LA), plus Tencent's China-side "AI Data 部".
  3. TikTok ADSO *Vendor Management Analyst / Partner Operation Manager / Partner Program Manager* (Singapore). This is more relevant for multilingual workforce and Trust & Safety annotation than for PhD-level expert data.
  4. Rakuten Asia *TPM, LLM data* (Singapore).
  5. DeepSeek's dedicated procurement post (Beijing/Hangzhou; not verified).
- **The ByteDance role description reads like a supplier-qualification checklist.** Its supplier categories are SFT, RM, Eval, Multimodal and Agentic Workflow, and the role tracks quarterly market intelligence on external resources and total operating cost. PathWize could map its medical, legal, finance and European-language offers onto these five categories.
- **Mandarin is a recurring hard requirement in buyer roles** (#1, #2, #7, #8). The roles also describe bridging "CN-based research teams and overseas vendors". This suggests that specifications and final decisions sit in China while Singapore and US teams act as the vendor interface. PathWize would likely need Mandarin-capable BD or a Singapore partner.
- **In-sourcing risk:** ByteDance is building its own overseas expert pool (Expert Community Analyst, Xpert). European experts who are hard to recruit, contract and pay compliantly (GDPR, EU labour and tax rules) are the gap a vendor can fill. This is an assessment, not a finding.
- **Opportunity window (assessment):** Forbes and others reported in Aug 2026 that US vendors sell about USD 500m a year of data to Chinese labs, and those vendors now face political scrutiny (see Q2). That could make an EU-native vendor more attractive to Chinese buyers. It also brings reputational and regulatory exposure that PathWize should assess.

## Q2. Which Asian companies directly hire European-language or Western-domain experts for AI training, and at what pay?

### Takeaway
Direct hiring of **European-language** experts by Asian AI companies is visible mainly at **TikTok in London, Dublin and Kuala Lumpur**. These roles are German-, Spanish-, Italian- and French-speaking "AI Data Operation", "Content Quality" and "Model Data QA" roles, several hired as **"Third-party Associates"**, meaning through staffing vendors. They involve RLHF and LLM training data. For **domain experts**, Chinese platforms (ByteDance Xpert, Alibaba Siriser, Ant Afu) recruit mostly Chinese professionals at 100–500 RMB (about USD 15–74) per task. ByteDance's Singapore team is explicitly building an "overseas expert ecosystem". Forbes reports that Chinese labs buy about USD 500m a year of human data from US vendors. Those vendors, Mercor among them, advertise German medical-expert gigs at USD 65–120/h. So unmet Western-expert demand is being met through vendors, not through direct job ads.

## Q3. Which buyer channels (supplier portals, data exchanges, conferences, RFPs) are documented and accessible to a European vendor?

### Takeaway
Supplier portals exist but their registration procedures are not public. supplier.bytedance.com hosts ByteDance's Business Partner Code of Conduct, and no Tencent or Alibaba supplier-onboarding page for AI data was found. China's data exchanges are building **international zones**. Shanghai's has more than 20 international data merchants and more than 100 listed products, and at the 2026 Global Data Merchants Conference (31 Oct 2026) it launched cross-listing with Astana alongside D&B, Dow Jones, EIU and others.
