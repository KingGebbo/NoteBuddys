Scope note and method: The research covered HKEX filings (MiniMax English prospectus downloaded and searched in full), Chinese and English press, Wikipedia (only for funding and listing status, with the underlying sources named where Wikipedia gave them), and job aggregators. The main aggregator was DataHub (datahub.ac.cn), a third-party site that mirrors BOSS直聘, Moka and Feishu postings. Where a detail page was opened, the original BOSS, Moka or Feishu URL is given. Where only the DataHub list page was seen, the posting is marked "DataHub list only, original not verified". LinkedIn was not opened directly. The Moonshot Singapore LinkedIn posting is known only through Reuters, Straits Times and RecordOwl coverage.

The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. After that, only known URLs were fetched. As a result, Galbot, Fourier, Galaxea, Leju and X Square job pages, the smaller medical-AI and legal-AI startups (Yidu, Medlinker, United Imaging Intelligence, Ant AQ, Powerlaw), Infinigence and Genspark funding are thin or missing. They are listed under Gaps.

## Q1. Company profiles: (a) data needs, (b) job postings, (c) channel, (d) funding, (e) US-list status, (f) fit for PathWize

### Takeaway
The strongest new buying signals beyond what the client already knew come from four companies:
- **Moonshot** has a dedicated procurement function for overseas data vendors. One role explicitly seeks global and overseas suppliers for pretraining, SFT, RL and multimodal data, and requires English and independent overseas negotiation.
- **Shengshu (Vidu)** has a "data supply chain lead" building a *global* multimodal supplier ecosystem. It also wants native reviewer pools for German, Italian, Spanish, Russian and Korean, which are managed through outsourcing vendors.
- **MiniMax**'s HKEX prospectus confirms that it buys commercial datasets under procurement agreements, uses outsourced labeling paid by volume, and "engage[s] domain experts". It has earmarked about HK$65M per year for 2026–2030 for "data procurement, data labeling, and compliance". It also recruits senior domain experts (economics, finance, life sciences, chemistry, maths, law) as Fellows, with London and San Francisco among the office options.
- **Baichuan** has the closest domain overlap with PathWize's pilots: part-time dermatology image evaluation experts, part-time radiology (X-ray/CT/MR) annotators, oncology reviewers and medical model evaluators. All of these roles are China- and Beijing-based and require Chinese clinical credentials.

Embodied-AI firms rely overwhelmingly on domestic, state-subsidised training grounds and cheap in-house or crowdsourced egocentric collection. There is little evidence that they buy from foreign vendors.

## Q7. HKEX prospectus disclosures on data procurement spending and suppliers

### Takeaway
MiniMax's prospectus is the most explicit document found. It confirms commercial dataset procurement under contract (with provenance warranties), outsourced labeling billed by volume, and engagement of domain experts. It allocates about HK$64.9M per year for 2026–2030 to "data procurement, data labeling, and compliance". It names no data supplier, because its top five suppliers are cloud and marketing vendors. Zhipu discloses "data cleaning and large model evaluation service providers" and outsourced annotation as supplier categories, also without names.

### Cited Findings
- MiniMax quotes (pp. 285, 301, 317) and the use-of-proceeds table (pp. 467–468) are given in Q1 — [MiniMax prospectus (English)](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1231/2025123100025.pdf); [Chinese version](https://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1231/2025123100026_c.pdf)
- MiniMax top-5 suppliers: 2022 US$4.5M (63.9%), 2023 US$49.8M (63.0%), 2024 US$149.0M (57.3%), 9M 2025 US$143.6M (62.5%). The suppliers are mostly cloud providers plus Singapore marketing firms (p. 297) — [prospectus](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/1231/2025123100025.pdf)
- Zhipu:
  - Supplier categories: (i) compute, (ii) hardware, (iii) R&D support such as data cleaning and model evaluation, (iv) marketing. The top-5 supplier share was 54.5%, 53.6%, 47.3% and 50.2% (2022 to H1 2025).
  - Data annotation is outsourced to third parties.
  - R&D spend: RMB 2.2B (2024) and RMB 1.59B (H1 2025).
  - Source: [prospectus mirror](https://saranormous.github.io/filing-websites/zhipu/); [Zhidx](https://zhidx.com/p/522919.html)

### Inferences
- ASSESSMENT: At about US$8M per year, MiniMax's data procurement and labeling budget is small next to compute but material for a niche expert vendor. Expert-generated data may also sit inside R&D staff costs: the Fellowship is funded from the HK$763.7M R&D-talent allocation.
