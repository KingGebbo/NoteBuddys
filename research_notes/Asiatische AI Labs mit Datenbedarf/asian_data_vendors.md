## Q1: Who are the relevant Asian vendors, and which serve Chinese/Asian labs while also selling internationally? (vendor profiles)

### Takeaway
Three China-linked vendors serve Chinese model builders and also have meaningful international revenue:
- **Haitian Ruisheng/Dataocean AI**: customers include Zhipu, Meta, Alibaba, Tencent, Moonshot and MiniMax. It is building North America, Europe and Japan/Korea sales teams.
- **Appen China**: H1 FY26 revenue of USD 76.2M (+80%). Its growth is explicitly linked to "supporting international expansion for Chinese technology companies".
- **Datatang/Nexdata**: overseas revenue of CNY 103M (28% of the total), served from US, Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore and Korea subsidiaries, with no European entity.

In Korea, **Flitto** is the clearest international seller. It earned KRW 35.9bn in 2025, about 95% of its core data business goes to US big tech, and it has a backlog of about KRW 31bn. In India, the expert-network competitors (iMerit/EXL Scholars, Deccan AI/Soul AI, Shaip/Ubiquity) are consolidating into larger IT/BPO groups.
